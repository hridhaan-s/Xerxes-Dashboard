    What this actually measures

    A webcam sees you from the front. That makes four things genuinely measurable: how high your head sits above your shoulder line, how far it drifts off the midline, how much your head and shoulders roll, and how your distance to the camera changes.

    It cannot see your lumbar curve or your true cervical angle, so Xerxes does not claim to. The distance figure is anchored to the one measurement you supply; everything else is a ratio against your own shoulder width, which is why it behaves the same on a 480p laptop camera and a 1080p external one.

    When you turn your head, head metrics are suspended rather than scored — glancing at a second monitor is not a posture fault.

    Video frames are processed by WebAssembly in this tab and are never uploaded, recorded or sent anywhere. The pose model is fetched once from a public CDN; after that the page works offline.
